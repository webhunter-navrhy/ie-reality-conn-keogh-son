Description

Keogh Auctioneers are delighted to offer for Sale by Private Treaty this Detached Bungalow on circa 0.88 acres with a substantial Detached Garage/Workshop.

The property is superbly located just off the N25 within meters of Pilltown Church and within 4 miles of the very pretty coastal village of Ardmore. This area offers the best of both country and coastal living and is a short drive or cycle or indeed within walking distance of a selection of breathtaking coves and beaches including Ardmore, Whiting Bay, Caliso Bay, Ferrypoint and Youghal. The large seaside towns of both Youghal and Dungarvan are a easy drive or on the Local Link Bus Route.

The property is nestled on a level site of circa 0.88 acres (circa 0.356 hectares) of mature landscaped gardens, driveway, rear patio and yard.

The Detached Bungalow itself has a handsome red brick exterior and presents to market in Turnkey condition. The property is ideal for family living with well proportioned rooms and an abundance of large windows giving the home a light filled airy atmosphere. Internal accommodation includes a welcoming and bright entrance hall with the Living area on the Left Hand Side and consisting of Living Room with Open Fire, Dining Room, Family Room, Home Office, Kitchen which is fully fitted, Utility, Family Bathroom, Ensuite Shower Shower, Guest WC and Four Bedrooms.

A key selling feature with this property is the substantial Detached Garage/Workshop which extends to circa 190 square meters.

This is a property which will not fail to impress and will appeal to the discerning buyer searching for a detached property which perfectly combines the best of country and coastal living but within easy access of urban areas and amenities.

Viewing is highly recommended
